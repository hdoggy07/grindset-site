Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy describes what Grindset actually does with your data — written to match the app's real behavior, not a generic template. If anything here ever stops being true of the app's actual code, this policy is updated at the same time.

The short version

Grindset works entirely on your own phone with no account at all. Everything you log — exercises, sets, weights, body weight, nutrition, your goal and settings — is stored only on your device and never sent anywhere. Creating an account is entirely optional and is only needed for one feature: adding friends and sharing a finished workout with them. Nothing is ever sold, shared with advertisers, or used to serve you ads — there are no ads or third-party trackers in this app at all.

What's stored on your device only

Without an account, or for anyone who never opens the Friends tab, all of the following lives only in your phone's local storage and is never transmitted anywhere by this app:

  • Every exercise, workout session, and logged set (weight, reps, RIR)
  • Sport/cardio sessions you log
  • Routines you save
  • Body-weight and body-measurement logs
  • Nutrition logs
  • Your goal, targets, and Coach history
  • App settings (units, bar weight, rest timer, gym/equipment context)

This data stays on your device until you delete it yourself (Settings → "Clear all data"), export it yourself (Settings → "Export your data"), or uninstall the app. Nobody — including whoever made this app — can see, access, or recover this data; it never leaves your phone.

What an account adds (entirely optional)

Creating an account (Friends tab → sign up) is the only way any data leaves your device, and only the following is involved:

  • Account data: your email address, a password (handled by our backend provider's authentication system — this app never sees or stores your actual password), your chosen username, display name, and the training goal you picked. This is stored so you can sign in again and so friends can find you by username.
  • Friend connections: who you've sent or received friend requests with, and who you're currently connected with.
  • Shared workouts: only workouts you explicitly tap "Share with friends" on, after finishing them — full detail (exercises, sets, weights, reps, duration, personal records). Nothing is ever shared automatically.
  • Kudos and comments: given or left on a shared workout, visible only within the same circle that can see the workout itself.

Your goal is visible only to you — even after adding friends, your goal is never shown to anyone else; friend cards show only your name. Body weight, nutrition logs, and calorie/macro targets are never synced or shared under any circumstance — those stay phone-only, permanently, with or without an account.

A shared workout, a kudos, or a comment is visible only to you and to friends you've mutually accepted — never to the public, and never to a pending or declined connection.

Who can see your account data

Nobody but you and your accepted friends, and only the specific things described above. This app's backend enforces this as real database rules (not just app-side logic that could have a bug): a friend request that hasn't been accepted grants no visibility, and even an accepted friend never sees your goal, body weight, or nutrition data — only workouts you chose to share, plus reactions to those.

Third-party processors

Account data (when you choose to create an account) is stored using Supabase, a backend infrastructure provider, over an encrypted connection. Supabase does not have permission to use this data for its own purposes; it is a processor, not a data controller. No other third-party service — no analytics platform, no advertising network, no crash-reporting or tracking SDK — is used by this app.

Deleting your data

  • Local data (everything if you've never made an account, or anything logged outside the Friends feature even if you have): Settings → "Clear all data," which deletes it permanently from your device.
  • Your account: Friends → "Delete account" permanently deletes your account, profile, friend connections, shared workouts, and any kudos or comments you've given or received — immediately and irreversibly. This does not touch anything stored locally on your phone.

Children's privacy

Grindset is not directed at, or knowingly used to collect information from, children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us using the information below and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If what this app collects or does with data ever changes, this policy will be updated to match before that change ships, and the "last updated" date above will reflect it.
